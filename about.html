About me

I design the marks, print, and pictures a business is remembered by.

I am PixelCraft, a graphic designer in Kumasi. The practice started with close, careful work: sitting with a business, finding the idea worth repeating, then drawing the pieces that make it visible.

From logos and packaging to posters and social templates, the aim stays the same. Make something beautiful, make it clear, and make the next step easy.
