Profile

PixelCraft is a graphic designer in Kumasi, Ghana. The work covers logos, print, packaging, and social graphics as one practice, so a business can move from an idea to a finished visual without the look splitting apart.

Selected work

  • Adinkra Atelier. Fashion wordmark, labels, and a printed lookbook.
  • Gold Coast Table. Restaurant identity, monogram, and menu design.
  • Safoa Wellness. Calm mark, packaging, and poster set.
  • Sika Nest. Savings-brand logo and social templates.
  • Harmattan House. Architecture stationery, brochure, and posters.
  • Kejetia Nights. Night-market campaign across poster, social, and motion.
